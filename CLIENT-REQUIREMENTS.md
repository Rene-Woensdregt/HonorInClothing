# Honor In Clothing — Client Requirements

## 1. Project Overview

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Brand Name | Honor In Clothing |
| Tagline | Honor in everything you do. |
| Industry | Premium Active Lifestyle Apparel |
| Platform | Shopify |
| Reference Site | Vuori Clothing (https://vuoriclothing.com) |

**Mission:** Honor In Clothing is a premium active lifestyle apparel brand built around honor, character, integrity, hard work, personal responsibility, service, and legacy. The brand is designed for individuals who live with purpose and believe that how you live matters.

---

## 2. Target Audience

- Active lifestyle men, women, and youth
- Outdoor enthusiasts
- Veterans and first responders
- Service-minded supporters
- Tradespeople
- Entrepreneurs
- Hardworking individuals and families
- Customers who value integrity, honor, and legacy

---

## 3. Website Goals

1. Establish a premium, trustworthy brand presence
2. Showcase apparel collections professionally
3. Drive online sales through Shopify
4. Build an email subscriber list
5. Share the Honor In Clothing mission and story
6. Create a platform that can grow with future products and collections
